## Key Findings

- System captures more than license plates.
    
- Vehicle make, model, color, and unique identifiers are recorded.
    
- Annual audits are required.
    
- Officers must verify alerts before enforcement action.
    
- Flock-hosted data retention is 30 days.
    
- Boone County may retain data longer.
    
- Policy prohibits sharing for federal immigration enforcement purposes.
