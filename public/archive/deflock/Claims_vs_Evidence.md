# Claims vs Evidence

## Supported Claims

### Claim

System captures more than license plates.

**Status:** Supported

**Evidence:**  
Policy 450 states the system captures license plates along with vehicle make, model, color, and unique vehicle identifiers.

**Source:**  
[[26-164_BCSO_Policy_450_ALPRs.pdf]]

---

### Claim

The system may be used without individualized suspicion.

**Status:** Supported

**Evidence:**  
Policy 450 states that reasonable suspicion or probable cause is not required before using an ALPR system during routine patrol operations or criminal investigations.

**Source:**  
[[26-164_BCSO_Policy_450_ALPRs.pdf]]

---

### Claim

ALPR alerts alone should not justify enforcement action.

**Status:** Supported

**Evidence:**  
Policy 450 requires officers to verify alerts, confirm vehicle descriptors, and obtain a lawful basis before taking enforcement action.

**Source:**  
[[26-164_BCSO_Policy_450_ALPRs.pdf]]

---

### Claim

Boone County performs annual ALPR audits.

**Status:** Supported

**Evidence:**  
Policy 450 requires the Sheriff or designee to conduct at least one audit annually and retain audit documentation.

**Source:**  
[[26-164_BCSO_Policy_450_ALPRs.pdf]]

---

### Claim

ALPR system access is auditable.

**Status:** Supported

**Evidence:**  
Policy 450 states that users must have individual credentials and that system access is fully auditable.

**Source:**  
[[26-164_BCSO_Policy_450_ALPRs.pdf]]

---

### Claim

Flock-hosted ALPR data is retained for 30 days.

**Status:** Supported

**Evidence:**  
Policy 450 states that Flock Safety purges hosted ALPR data after 30 days.

**Source:**  
[[26-164_BCSO_Policy_450_ALPRs.pdf]]

---

### Claim

Boone County may retain ALPR-related records longer than Flock.

**Status:** Supported

**Evidence:**  
Policy 450 states that relevant vehicle data may be retained according to county retention schedules.

**Source:**  
[[26-164_BCSO_Policy_450_ALPRs.pdf]]

---

### Claim

Data may be shared with other law enforcement agencies.

**Status:** Supported

**Evidence:**  
Policy 450 contains procedures governing the release of ALPR information to other law enforcement and prosecutorial agencies.

**Source:**  
[[26-164_BCSO_Policy_450_ALPRs.pdf]]

---

## Unsupported / Not Supported Claims

### Claim

ICE has direct access for immigration enforcement.

**Status:** Not Supported

**Evidence:**  
Policy 450 states that Boone County does not permit ALPR data sharing for federal immigration enforcement purposes involving ICE or CBP.

**Source:**  
[[26-164_BCSO_Policy_450_ALPRs.pdf]]

---

### Claim

Officers can stop vehicles solely because an ALPR alert occurred.

**Status:** Not Supported

**Evidence:**  
Policy 450 requires verification procedures and states that an alert alone is insufficient for enforcement action.

**Source:**  
[[26-164_BCSO_Policy_450_ALPRs.pdf]]

---
