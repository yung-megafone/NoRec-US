# Verified Findings

## Boone County Sheriff's Office

### Confirmed through [[26-164_BCSO_FOIA]] / [[26-164_BCSO_Policy_450_ALPRs.pdf]]
- Uses Flock Safety ALPR technology.
- Policy references capture of make, model, color, and unique identifiers.
- Policy requires alert verification before enforcement action.
- Policy references annual audits.
- Flock-hosted retention listed as 30 days.
- County may retain relevant data longer.
- Policy states ALPR data is not shared for federal immigration enforcement purposes.
